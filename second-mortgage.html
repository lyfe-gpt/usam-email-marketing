Why a 2nd, not a refi

Your equity is working capital.

If you bought or refinanced when rates were low, a cash-out refi would reprice your entire balance just to reach the equity. A 2nd trust deed sits behind your first, so you only pay today's rate on the money you actually pull out.

We built this for investors and self-employed borrowers who don't fit the traditional box. We underwrite the property and the equity, not a stack of W-2s. Use the proceeds for a down payment on the next deal, a rehab budget, or business capital.
