The annual problem

Good tax strategy, bad loan file.

Every February the same thing happens. Investors and self-employed borrowers file returns full of depreciation, expenses, and write-offs. That lowers the tax bill, which is the point. It also lowers the AGI the bank underwrites from, so the same return that saved you money gets your loan declined.

We underwrite the asset instead. Here are three ways to qualify without handing over a single tax return.
1
DSCR The property's rent qualifies. If the rent covers the payment, the deal works. Your personal income never comes up.
2
Bank Statement Your deposits qualify. We read 12-24 months of real cash flow instead of the AGI your write-offs shrank.
3
Hard Money The asset qualifies. Equity and exit drive the approval, so time-sensitive deals close in days, not months.

We are not licensed CPAs. Talk to a CPA about the right tax strategy for your situation.
