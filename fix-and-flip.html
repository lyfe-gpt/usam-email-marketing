How it works

One loan covers the buy and the budget.

Most flippers do not lose deals on price. They lose them on speed and on cash tied up in the last project. Our Fix and Flip loan puts 90% of the purchase and up to 100% of the rehab budget on our balance sheet, so your capital stays free for earnest money, crews, and the next contract.

Payments are interest-only for the 6-month term, so your carrying cost stays predictable while the project is underway.
