Hard Money vs Conventional Loan

Speed and flexibility versus the lowest-cost long-term money. Conventional loans offer lower costs for move-in-ready properties with documented income. Hard money delivers speed and flexibility for deals banks will not finance quickly.

Common questions

What borrowers ask us.

Why is hard money faster than a conventional loan?

We underwrite the deal, not a stack of personal income documentation, so there is no waiting on tax transcripts or committee review. Most USA Mortgage hard money loans close in 5 to 7 days.

Can I refinance hard money into a conventional loan?

Yes. That is the typical exit: use hard money to close and stabilize the property, then refinance into lower-cost long-term conventional or DSCR debt once it will pass a standard appraisal.
