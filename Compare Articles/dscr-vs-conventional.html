DSCR Loan vs Conventional Loan

Qualify on the property, or on your personal income.

Apply now →

DSCR loans qualify based on rental income coverage without personal documentation and allow LLC ownership with no property caps. Conventional loans rely on personal income and DTI ratios, typically offer lower rates, but restrict the number of financed properties.
DSCR Conventional
Qualification basis Property cash flow (DSCR) Personal income and DTI
Documentation None required Tax returns, W-2s, pay stubs
Ownership structure LLC friendly Usually personal name
Property limits No set cap Often capped (around 10 financed)
Typical rate Higher than conventional Lowest available
Best for Self-employed / scaling investors W-2 earners with few rentals
Bottom line

If the deal has to clear your personal DTI and you only own a handful of financed properties, conventional will usually be your lowest-cost option. Once your write-offs, self-employment income, or property count start working against you on a personal application, DSCR lets the property carry the deal instead, in an LLC, with no set cap on how many you hold.
Frequently asked

Is a DSCR loan rate higher than conventional?

Usually, yes. DSCR trades a slightly higher rate, ours starts at 5.5% interest-only on a 30-year fixed, for skipping personal income documentation and property caps entirely.

Can I hold a DSCR loan in an LLC?

Yes. DSCR loans are LLC friendly by design, which is part of why investors scaling a portfolio use them over conventional financing, which is usually held in a personal name.
