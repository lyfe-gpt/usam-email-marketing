Stop losing spreads you already earned.

You found the deal, negotiated the contract, and lined up the end buyer. The only thing standing between you and your assignment fee is the cash to close the A-B leg. That is exactly what transactional funding is for: short-term capital that exists for hours, not months, so the double close happens on schedule.

We underwrite the transaction, not you. If your B-to-C contract is signed and the end buyer's funds are confirmed, we wire the A-B purchase. And if you need a proof-of-funds letter to get your next offer taken seriously, we issue those same day.

How it works
1 Get the property under contract (A-B). Send us your purchase contract and your signed end-buyer contract (B-C).
2 We fund the A-B close. Full purchase amount wired to title, typically within 1-3 days of a complete file.
3 Your B-C buyer closes. We are repaid the same day from the second closing, and your spread wires to you.
