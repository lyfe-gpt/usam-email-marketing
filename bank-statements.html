How it works

Built for how self-employed investors actually get paid.

If you run your own business, your tax returns understate what you actually make. That is the point of good tax strategy, and it is exactly why banks keep turning you down.

We look at the money moving through your accounts instead. Send us 12-24 months of bank statements, personal or business, across multiple accounts if that is how you operate. We average your deposits as income and underwrite from there. Buy, refinance, or pull cash out of what you already own, on a 30-year term.
