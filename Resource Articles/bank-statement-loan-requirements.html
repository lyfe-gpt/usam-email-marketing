Bank Statement / No-Doc

What it actually takes to qualify on your deposits.

A bank statement loan is not a lower bar, it is a different one. Here is exactly what USAM checks: credit, deposit history, down payment, and reserves, so you can walk in knowing where you stand before you apply.

Credit and paperwork

Bank statements in, tax returns out.

Bring 12 to 24 months of business or personal bank statements, whichever tells your income story most accurately. That is the entire income file. No W-2s, no tax returns, no pay stubs. Credit runs around 660 or higher, with the best pricing reserved for scores above 700.
How income is calculated

Your deposits, averaged and read as income.

We average your deposits over the statement period, then apply an expense factor to estimate net business income from the gross. A strong borrower with heavy write-offs is not punished for running a tax-efficient business. The number on your return and the money actually moving through your accounts are two different things, and this program is built around the second one.
Down payment and reserves

Plan for skin in the game, both ways.

Expect a down payment of roughly 20% to 30%, plus reserves set aside to carry the property after closing. Lenders want to see that the deal works even before day one, not just that the deposits look good on paper.
