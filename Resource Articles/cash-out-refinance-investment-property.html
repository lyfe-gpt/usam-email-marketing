Rental / DSCR

Your rental has equity. Put it back to work.

A cash-out refinance replaces your current loan with a larger one and hands you the difference in cash, turning equity you already own into capital for the next deal. On a rental, a DSCR cash-out refinance qualifies on the property's rent, not your tax returns.

Apply now →
How it works

One new loan, one payout.

The mechanics are simple: your new lender pays off the existing loan balance, and you keep the surplus as cash at closing. With a DSCR refinance, that new loan is underwritten against the rent the property brings in, so a complicated tax return or a season of heavy write-offs never enters the conversation. If the rent supports the new payment, you qualify.
When investors use it

Three deals, the same move.

✓ Closing out a BRRRR. Once a rehab is rented and stabilized, the cash-out refinance is what returns your original capital so it can fund the next purchase.
✓ Pulling equity from a rental you already own. Years of appreciation and paydown sit idle as equity until you refinance and free it for a down payment elsewhere.
✓ Replacing a bridge loan. Move off short-term debt and onto a 30-year fixed DSCR loan, pulling cash out in the same closing.
How much you can pull

Three things set the number.

The cash you can pull depends on the property's current value, the loan-to-value ceiling on the new loan, and, on a DSCR refinance, whether the rent supports the new payment at that loan size. The stronger the appraisal and the stronger the rent, the more of that equity converts to cash in your hands.
