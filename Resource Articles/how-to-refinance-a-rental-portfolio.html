How to refinance a rental portfolio.

If you own several rentals on separate mortgages, a portfolio refinance rolls them into one loan with one payment. You qualify on the combined cash flow, pull cash out of the pooled equity, and keep the freedom to sell any single door. Here is how it works.

Apply now →
What it is

Many mortgages, one loan.

A portfolio refinance consolidates several separate rental mortgages into a single loan with a single payment. Instead of tracking a stack of due dates, renewal cycles, and lenders, you manage one payment, one renewal, and one point of contact across the whole group.
How you qualify

The portfolio qualifies, not your W-2.

Rather than underwriting each property on its own, we look at the combined cash flow and overall leverage of the group, the same way a DSCR loan works across the whole portfolio. Bring the rent roll and operating history, current values and debt on each door, and a clean credit profile with reserves. There is typically no personal income documentation and no cap on the number of financed properties.
✓  One loan, one payment ✓  Qualify on portfolio cash flow
✓  Cash out of pooled equity ✓  No cap on financed doors
Cash out and flexibility

Pull equity, keep your exits open.

The pooled structure lets you extract equity built up across every property and turn it into buying power for the next acquisition. And a well-built portfolio loan carries a release clause, so you can sell any single door without unwinding or refinancing the entire loan. You consolidate for simplicity without giving up the flexibility to reshape the portfolio as it grows.
