How fast can a hard money loan close?

About 5 to 7 days, versus 30 to 60 or more for a bank. Hard money is asset-based, so the underwriting is about the deal, not your personal income. Speed is the whole point: it wins the deals a bank timeline would lose.

Why it is faster

The deal is the underwriting, not your tax returns.

A bank underwrites you: income, W-2s, debt-to-income, and weeks of document back-and-forth. Hard money underwrites the asset. Because the loan is secured by the property and its exit, we skip the parts of the process that stretch a bank close to 30 to 60 days or more. The result is a close measured in days, not months.
