What credit score do you need for a DSCR loan?

A DSCR loan qualifies on the property's rent, not your tax returns. There is no personal income test. But your credit score still sets the rate you get and how much leverage you can take. Here is how it works, and where the USA Mortgage line sits.

Apply now
The short answer

USA Mortgage lends from a 640.

Most DSCR lenders draw the line at 660 to 680. USA Mortgage starts at 640, so a credit profile that gets turned away elsewhere can still get a rental deal done here. Because there is no personal income test on a DSCR loan, your score is the main read a lender has on borrower risk, which is exactly why the minimum matters.
How credit moves your terms

Credit is a lever, not a gate.

A stronger score signals lower risk, which earns you a better rate and a higher loan-to-value. A weaker score does not stop the deal; it moves you to a lower rate tier and asks for a larger down payment. Clearing the minimum gets you in the door. Where you land above it decides the price and the leverage.
Before you apply

Three ways to strengthen the file.

✓ Pay down revolving balances. Clear collections and lower your credit-card utilization before you apply. It is the fastest lever on a score.
✓ Bring a larger down payment. A lower loan-to-value offsets a thinner credit profile and can move you into a better rate tier.
✓ Lead with a strong-cash-flow property. More rent cushion above the payment offsets a lower score. The deal and the credit work together.
