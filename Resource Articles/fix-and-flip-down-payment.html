How much do you actually put down on a flip?

Less than most investors think. We fund up to 90% of the purchase and up to 100% of the rehab, so your cash-in is roughly the 10% down plus closing. Here is how the math works, and how a strong file shrinks it further.

The math

Your down payment is about 10% of the purchase.

We finance up to 90% of the purchase price, so on most deals you bring in the remaining 10% as your down payment. The rehab is a separate line: we fund up to 100% of it and reimburse the work through draws as it is completed. That structure keeps renovation costs off your down payment entirely.

On a $250K purchase, that is roughly $25K down. Compare that to buying the property and the rehab in cash, and you can see why leverage is what lets flippers run more than one project at a time.

What to budget beyond the down payment

✓  Closing costs: title, valuation, legal ✓  Lender fees (points)
✓  Rehab carry, floated before draws ✓  A reserve for overruns
Shrink the cash-in

A strong file lowers what you bring. Higher credit and a well-supported after-repair value push you to the top of our leverage range, so less of your own money goes in per deal. Buying well below market builds equity that offsets the down payment, and when the ARV supports an interest reserve you can defer payments until the property sells.
