What is a blanket loan?

Once a portfolio grows past a few doors, tracking separate mortgages starts to cost you time and equity. A blanket loan rolls multiple properties into one loan and one payment. Here is how it works, and when it is worth the switch.

Apply now →
The short version

One loan, multiple properties, one payment.

A blanket loan, also called a portfolio loan, is a single mortgage secured by several properties at once instead of a separate mortgage for each one. The lender consolidates the properties collectively, and you manage one loan and one payment instead of a stack of them. Most blanket loans include a release clause, so you can sell an individual property and pay down that portion without unwinding the entire loan.
Why investors use it

Fewer moving parts, more room to grow.

✓ Simplicity. One loan, one payment, one renewal date, instead of tracking a separate mortgage for every door in the portfolio.
✓ Capital efficiency. Consolidating properties into one loan can free up equity you can put toward the next acquisition.
✓ Built to scale. Designed for investors moving past a handful of properties into a real portfolio.
When it makes sense

Best once you already hold several doors.

Blanket loans fit best for investors who already hold several stabilized rentals and want to streamline financing across the portfolio. If you are still acquiring properties one at a time, an individual DSCR loan is usually the better move until you have built up enough inventory to justify consolidating.
