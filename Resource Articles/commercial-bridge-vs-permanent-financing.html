Commercial bridge vs permanent financing.

Both are debt on the same property, but they solve different problems. Here is how to tell which one your deal needs right now, and how the two work together.

Apply now →
The short answer

Two tools, two jobs.

A commercial bridge loan is fast, flexible, and short-term: built to acquire or reposition a property that does not yet qualify for permanent debt. Permanent financing costs less and long-term: built for a property that has already reached stable, documented income. Most commercial deals use both, in sequence, not one or the other.

Which one fits your deal.

Use a bridge
•  Closing on a time-sensitive opportunity
•  Repositioning through lease-up or renovation
•  Cash-out to fund the next deal
Use permanent
•  Property has stable, documented income
•  You want the lowest long-term rate and term
•  You plan to hold the asset, not flip it
