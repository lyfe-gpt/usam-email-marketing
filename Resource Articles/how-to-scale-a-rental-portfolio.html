How to scale a rental portfolio.

Most investors do not hit a wall because they run out of good deals. They hit a wall because conventional lenders cap how many financed properties they will count and weigh every new loan against your personal debt-to-income ratio. Scaling past a few doors is a financing problem, and it has a financing answer.

The wall most investors hit

Stop qualifying as a consumer. Start qualifying as a business.

Conventional financing was built for a homeowner, not a portfolio. Once you cross a handful of financed properties, banks start counting every mortgage against your personal debt-to-income ratio, no matter how much rent those properties actually bring in. The fix is not a bigger paycheck. It is business-purpose loans that qualify on the properties themselves, not on you.
The financing playbook

Three tools that scale with you.

✓ DSCR loans. Each property qualifies on its own rent against its own payment, with no cap on how many financed properties you already hold. Buy the next door without your last four purchases counting against you.
✓ The BRRRR method. Buy, rehab, rent, refinance, repeat. A cash-out refinance on a stabilized property recycles your capital back out so the next acquisition does not wait on a new pile of savings.
✓ Portfolio and blanket loans. Once you are holding several doors, consolidating them under one loan means one payment, one closing, and one lender to manage instead of a stack of separate notes.
Structure it right

Hold properties in an LLC.

DSCR and portfolio loans can close directly in your entity, which keeps liability separated property by property and keeps qualification centered on the deal instead of your personal file. We close DSCR loans in the LLC from 5.5% interest-only, with a DSCR floor of 0.75, so a growing portfolio never slows the next closing down.
