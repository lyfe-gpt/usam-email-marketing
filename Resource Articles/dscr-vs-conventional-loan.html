DSCR vs conventional loan: which fits your next rental?

Both can finance a rental, but they qualify two different things. A conventional loan qualifies you, your income, your tax returns, your debt-to-income. A DSCR loan qualifies the property and the rent it brings in. Here is how to pick the one that fits the deal in front of you.

Apply now →
The core difference

One qualifies you. The other qualifies the property.

A conventional loan runs on your personal financial picture. The lender pulls your tax returns, checks your debt-to-income ratio, and closes the loan in your personal name. It is usually the lower-cost money, but it is slower, it is personal, and it caps how many mortgages you can hold. A DSCR loan flips that. It asks one question about the property: does the rent cover the payment? No tax returns, closes in an LLC, and no limit on the number of properties you own.

How to choose

Cheapest rate, or a loan that scales?

If you have clean W-2 income, room in your debt-to-income ratio, and you only need one or two properties, conventional is usually the lower-cost path and worth the extra paperwork. Once you are self-employed, writing off income, holding properties in an LLC, or buying past the point a bank will keep lending, DSCR is what keeps you moving.
