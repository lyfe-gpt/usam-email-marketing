What is transactional funding?

Transactional funding covers the A-to-B leg of a wholesaler's double close. You buy from the seller and resell to your end buyer the same day, funded by us in between. Your signed B-to-C contract is the qualification, so there is no credit check and no income documentation.

The short version

Same-day money for the deal you have already resold.

When you have a property under contract to buy and already have an end buyer lined up, transactional funding bridges the two closings so you never use your own cash. It is short-term by design: funded and repaid the same day out of your buyer's proceeds. Because the exit is already signed, we qualify the deal, not your balance sheet.
How it works
1 You buy from the seller. This is the A-to-B close. We fund the purchase so the deal can close without your own capital tied up.
2 You resell to your end buyer. This is the B-to-C close, the same day. The signed B-to-C contract is your qualification, so there is no credit check and no income documentation.
3 The loan is repaid same day. It is a flat fee, repaid out of the B-to-C proceeds. Proof-of-funds letters are available up front so you can make offers with confidence.
