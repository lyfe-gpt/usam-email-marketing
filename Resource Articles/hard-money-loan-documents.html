What documents does a hard money loan need?

Hard money lending is asset-based, not income-based, so the file is shorter than a bank's. No tax returns, no W-2s, no pay stubs on most programs. Here is what USA Mortgage actually needs to fund your deal.

Apply now
1. The checklist

Seven items cover most files.

We are underwriting the property and the plan, not a pay stub. A complete file usually looks like this.

✓  Purchase contract (or payoff, for a refi) ✓  Scope of work and rehab budget
✓  Entity documents (LLC formation, operating agreement) ✓  Bank statements or proof of reserves
✓  Property insurance binder ✓  Valid ID and credit authorization
✓  Exit plan (sale or refinance)
2. What we skip

No income paperwork on most programs.

No tax returns, no W-2s, and no pay stubs on most asset-based programs. We are evaluating the property and the business plan, not your personal income, so a thin income history or a complicated tax return does not slow the file down the way it would at a bank.
3. Why the file matters

A complete file closes in days.

A complete file closes in days; a thin one drags. Title, insurance, and documentation completeness drive your closing timeline more than our decision does. Send everything on the checklist up front and there is nothing left to chase later in the week.
