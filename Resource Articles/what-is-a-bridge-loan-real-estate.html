What is a bridge loan?

Short-term financing that carries you from one milestone to the next: buy before you sell, reposition a value-add asset, or pay off a maturing loan, then refinance into permanent debt. Here is how it works, and when it is the right tool.

The short version

A loan built to close a gap in time.

A bridge loan is short-term financing that carries you from one milestone to the next. It gets you into the next property before the current one sells, funds the work on a value-add asset, or pays off a loan that is coming due, until a longer-term source of capital is ready. Terms run 12 to 36 months and are usually interest-only, so payments stay light while the plan plays out.

When a bridge is the right tool.

✓ Buy before you sell: secure the next property while your current one is still on the market.
✓ Reposition an asset: fund the improvements that stabilize a value-add deal and lift its value.
✓ Pay off a maturing loan: clear a note that is coming due, then refinance into permanent debt on your timeline.
