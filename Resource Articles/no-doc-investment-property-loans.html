No-doc investment property loans, explained.

A no-doc loan qualifies you on the property, your credit, and your reserves, not your tax returns. No W-2s, no income calculations, no assembling two years of paperwork. Here is how it works and who it fits.

What "no-doc" means

We skip income entirely.

A no-doc loan, also called no-ratio lending, qualifies you on property, credit, and reserves instead of income verification. There are no tax returns, no W-2s, and no income calculations. Because these are business-purpose loans on investment property, the consumer ability-to-repay rules that restrict no-doc home loans do not apply here.
Where it fits

Three ways to qualify without a tax return.

1 Bank statement loans qualify on 12 to 24 months of deposits, useful when your revenue is strong but your tax returns are not.
2 DSCR loans qualify on the property's rent versus the payment, so the asset does the qualifying instead of you.
3 No-doc loans skip income entirely and rely on assets and reserves, trading a higher rate and a larger down payment for speed and simplicity.
What it takes

Credit, reserves, and a bigger down payment.

Expect a credit profile typically around 660 or higher, reserves that show you can carry the property, and a down payment commonly in the 20% to 30% range. In exchange, you get a real rate in minutes, no obligation, and no hard credit pull to start.

It fits investors whose tax returns understate their real income, borrowers with complex finances, and anyone purchasing through an LLC who would rather not assemble a full income file.
