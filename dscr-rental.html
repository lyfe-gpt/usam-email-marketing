How it works

The property does the qualifying.

DSCR is the ratio of the property's rent to its monthly payment. We underwrite that number, so write-offs, self-employment income, and a complicated tax picture do not slow the deal down. Buy your next rental, or pull cash out of one you already own, on a 30-year fixed you never have to refinance.
